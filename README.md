# ndGUI for Spiritech

**A full Notum Dovvetech / DarkTech UI replacement for the Project Rubi-Ka *Spiritech* client.**
Near-black navy panels, steel-blue frames, a blue accent, square buttons. The classic ndGUI feel, rebuilt for the new client, and everything can be moved.

> 🐛 **Bugs, issues, ideas? Ask `thedenis` on the Project Rubi-Ka Discord.**
> Something broken or gone stock? Type **`/uidebug`** in chat: it lists every UI package, failed ones with
> their error. A screenshot of that (click the failed row) plus a screenshot of the problem helps a lot.

> Fan project. Not affiliated with Funcom or the Project Rubi-Ka team. Inspired by the classic
> ndGUI (Kitao's Dovvetech GUI, continued by Misat0 and Honorbound). It contains none of their
> textures or skins: every pixel here is generated by the scripts in this repo.

## What you get

**HUD**
- Slim **control center** strip on the bottom edge: menus, target cycling, NCU, credits, DEF/AGG slider.
- **Health / nano** bars by your character, numbers on a dark plate so they stay readable. **Double-click** one for its **Lock** (stays put) and **Flat** (horizontal, number inside) buttons. **XP / AXP** sit on the menubar (`XP  1,396 / 4,500`).
- **Compass** top centre: an outlined dotted strip with N / E / S / W that reads on light and dark ground, a green waypoint marker pinned to the edge when it's behind you, and the distance (`312 m`, `1.2 km`).
- **Target panels**: unit frames with profession portrait, level, exact HP (`941 / 941 (100%)`), a pale "damage just taken" ghost, low-HP pulse, nano, profession / side, distance and a reach dot. **Double-click** for **Lock**, **Size** (S / M / L) and **Compact**.
- **Floating combat numbers** over target panels and team frames (damage, bigger yellow crits, healing, misses), and **who your target is attacking** (`» You` in orange).
- **Nameplates**: clean names, a framed health bar in soft level colours (green, amber, red), corner ticks for selected (blue) / attacking (red), both when your target is attacking.
- **Docking column and minibar** (Options > Interface & HUD): right-click a window's title, move to the right-edge column, left-click to dock it.
- Smooth bar fills everywhere (no segment blocks).
- **Hotbars** with a DarkTech plate, recessed slots, a lit rail on the active bar, and a spinner that doubles as the drag handle.
- Restyled **NCU, target NCU, faction, chat config, raid bars, timer bars, team invite, sneak indicator, logout countdown**.

**Windows** (every `core.window` window gets the ndGUI frame)
Inventory, Wear, Stats, Skills, Nano Programs, Team, Raid, Friends, Pets, Perks, Trade, Tradeskills, Market, Info, NPC dialogue, Actions, Agg/Def, chat windows.

**Team** (AI-hand-made): every member is a unit frame like the target panels: profession portrait, exact HP with the damage ghost, nano, distance, leader in green, absent members greyed. Click to target, right-click menu, NCU strips as before. **Compact** shows name, level and health only.

**Missions and Info** (AI-hand-made, one package: Mission Hub)
- **Info keeps every mission briefing you look at** (cash, XP, zone, item reward, description), shared by all your characters. The Missions window reads the same notes.
- **Missions**: the list with time-left bars (blue, yellow under 30 min, red under 10) and a side pane with the chosen mission's briefing, pay, zone and reward. Folds to its title bar (`Missions (2) - 1:20`).

**Mission Terminal** (AI-hand-made, its own package)
- Offer rows instead of six bare icons, each with its pay, XP, playfield, QL and reward right away (`2,827 credits · 486 XP · Rome Blue · QL 120`, `Reward: Carbonum Plate Helmet (QL 118)`). A **watch list** lights matching offers gold (it searches the summary, playfield and reward) and logs them; four slider **presets** (left-click applies, right-click saves); roll counter and what chosen-reward rolls cost. An **offer map** beside the rows shows where each numbered offer is (watched ones gold, the selected one ringed, and you); click a number to pick it, drag to pan, wheel or `-` `+` to zoom, **Map** hides it. In Options, Mission terminal is this package. Info and Missions stay on Mission Hub.

**Damage Meter** (AI-hand-made; Control Center > Actions > **Addons** > Damage Meter)
- **Damage / Healing / Taken / Targets / Deaths** as bars: amount, per second, share. **Me / Team / Raid / All**.
- **Click a row**: abilities (weapon by damage type, Fling Shot, Burst..., nanos) with hits and biggest hit; who hit a target; the last 10 hits before a death.
- Live per-second graph, crit / miss / biggest hit / best 5 s, fight history (`<` `>`, last 10) and Overall, pets counted as you.

**Buff Watch** (AI-hand-made; Control Center > Actions > **Addons** > Buff Watch / Team Buffs)
- **Buff Watch**: your buffs and debuffs with icons, time left and draining bars (debuffs first in red, then soonest to end; amber under 1 min, red and blinking at the end), and what *you* put on your target (your DoTs, debuffs, or buffs on a friend). Right-click a buff: **Always show as a row**, **Don't warn about this buff** (team auras), **Hide this buff**.
- **Team Buffs**: one row per member, one column per buff anyone has; red cells are missing buffs (`Bob  -1`). Click a member to target them, then cast as usual (addons can't cast).
- **Alerts** under the compass: low health, a debuff landing, low nano, a long buff about to end. Toggle it from Buff Watch's title icon menu.

**Intel** (AI-hand-made; one package for chat, overhead combat text and zone notices, so they can share what they see)
- **Intel strip** (top left, drag by its grip): XP / alien XP / credits **per hour** this session, **time to your next level** (from the last 10 minutes), session clock. Hover for totals. **Alts**, **Log** and **0** (start the counters over) buttons. Turn the strip off under **Options > Interface > Intel**.
- **Alerts**: a banner for a **tell**, **your name** said in any channel, or a **watch word** (`WTS`, an item name…). The chat line gets a coloured `>` in front. Click the banner for the **Log**: this session's alerts and loot, newest first. The same Options page turns the banner off.
- **Alts**: every character you've played with ndGUI on this PC: profession, level, XP %, credits (and the total over all of them), last zone, when you last played it, and its **raid locks** (press **Raid Locks** in the Raid window once on each character; ndGUI reads the answer from chat). Click `x` twice to forget one.
- **"Which character has it?"**: every bag and bank window you open is remembered per character. Type in a bag's search box and a gold **`Alts: 2`** beside it lists where else that item is (hover it): other characters, your bank.
- **Overhead combat numbers** in ndGUI style with a shadow: quick hits on the same target **merge** into one growing number (`2,340 x5`), criticals and hits near your best of the session **pop** big and gold. Both toggles and the size are in Options > Combat > Floating combat text.
- **Zone notice** in ndGUI colours (it also tells Alts where you are).
- **Commands**, typed where only you see them: `/ndgui alts`, `/ndgui log`, `/ndgui hud` (hide/show the strip), `/ndgui reset`, `/ndgui watch <word>`, `/ndgui unwatch <word>`, `/ndgui words`. If the game sends unknown slash commands to the server instead of answering locally, send them as a tell to yourself (`/tell <you> ndgui alts`).

**Target Debuffs** (in Buff Watch; Control Center > Actions > **Addons** > Target Debuffs): your effects on the target as draining bars, red at the end. It **learns your rotation**: an effect of yours seen on two different enemies is one you keep up, and when your enemy target lacks it a pulsing red **REAPPLY** row shows. Right-click a row to stop (or start) tracking it; the title icon menu forgets the lot.

**Local Map** (AI-hand-made)
- Toolbar: zoom −/+ (and the mouse wheel), Me, Follow, Trail, Rings, Show (marker filters), Mini.
- Live world **X/Z**, and **cursor world position + distance** under the pointer.
- Breadcrumb **trail**, 25/50/100 m **range rings**, DarkTech markers, a live legend (Mobs / Players / Missions…).
- Calibrates its world scale by itself while you walk.

**Planet Map** (AI-hand-made)
- Toolbar: zoom with a level readout, Me, Mission, Follow, Route, Maps.
- **Mission distance and bearing** (`Mission 2.4 km NE`), a blue "you" beacon with a halo, a gold mission beacon and a dotted route between them.

**Everywhere**
- **Square 3-slice buttons**, also on the client's own screens (Options, login, character select…).
- **Lists**: right-click a column header to **move columns** left/right or **hide/show** them. Remembered per list.
- **Pins** show their state (lit blue key when pinned).
- Steel-blue **tabs, scrollbars, dropdowns, sliders, item slots**. Stock teal is retinted to steel.
- Crisp text at high UI scale (tested at 4.5K / 171 %): baked-text art was replaced with real labels.
- Every piece can be dragged, and positions are saved per character.
- **Snap to grid** while moving or resizing (see [Snap to grid](#snap-to-grid)), and a **Grid Overlay** (Control Center > Actions > **Addons** > Grid Overlay) that draws the grid and amber screen-centre lines while you arrange things. It takes no clicks; close it from the same menu.

## Install (short version)

Two ways in. Both use the same DarkTech HUD; the difference is how deep the skin goes.

### Spiritech Marketplace (in-game HUD)

1. Quit the game.
2. In the Spiritech Launcher, open **Addons** and install **ndGUI**.
3. Options → Interface & HUD → **UI packages** → pick the **Notum Dovvetech** providers; re-enter the character.

That is enough for the in-game HUD, DarkTech buttons, slot wells, compass, and maps. Details: [docs/marketplace-listing.md](docs/marketplace-listing.md).

### Install script (full atlas + Login/Options)

The player installer merges DarkTech art into the game's graphics atlas and squares Login / Options / character screens. No Python or exe: `install.bat` is plain PowerShell you can read in Notepad.

1. **Quit the game.**
2. Download **`ndgui-spiritech-<version>.zip`** from the **[latest release](../../releases)** and **unpack it** (right-click → *Extract All*).
3. Double-click **`install.bat`** in the unpacked folder and press **Enter** (option 1 = install).
   It finds the game by itself. If it can't, it opens a folder picker: choose the folder that holds `PRK.Client.exe`.
4. Start the game. If something still looks stock, log out to character select and back in once.

**Already on Marketplace?** Unpack the same player zip and run `install.ps1 -SkinLayouts` (or `-SkinLayouts -KeepProviders`) for atlas / Login-Options only, without redoing packages.

**Update:** run `install.bat` and pick **2) Update to the newest version**. It downloads the latest release from GitHub and installs it. Also re-run it after a game patch, which may put stock files back.
**Undo everything:** run `install.bat` and pick **4) Uninstall**. Every game file it touches is backed up first.

Linux/Mac, or building from source: `python install.py` (see [INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md)).

Full guide, options, updating after a client patch, and troubleshooting: **[INSTALL.md](INSTALL.md)**.

## Snap to grid

Windows and HUD pieces snap to a grid when you move or resize them (10 px unless you change it). It uses the game's own snapping switch: **Options > Interface & HUD > Windows and HUD > magnetism** above 0 turns on edge snapping and the grid; 0 turns both off. When another window's edge is in reach, it snaps to that edge instead.

**Change the grid size** (any whole number of pixels from 0 to 200; 0 = grid off). Quit the game first, then restart it afterwards. Updates and reinstalls keep your size.

- **Windows:** double-click `install.bat` and pick **5) Snap-to-grid size**. Or, in PowerShell in the unpacked folder:
  ```powershell
  .\install.ps1 -Grid 20
  ```
- **Linux / Mac** (or from source on Windows): in the ndGUI folder run `python install.py` and pick **5) Snap-to-grid size**, or:
  ```bash
  python install.py --grid 20
  python install.py --grid 20 --client "/path/to/PRK-AO-Native/client"   # if it can't find the game
  ```
- **By hand** (any system): edit `local SIZE = 10` near the top of `client/UI/Addons/ndgui-lib/lua/grid.lua`.

The Grid Overlay always draws the size you set (a small grid shows every 2nd / 3rd line so it stays readable).

## How it works (one paragraph)

Spiritech loads Lua/XML UI packages from `client/UI/Addons`. This repo holds **17 totally AI-hand-made packages** ([`addons/`](addons)) and the Python tools that derive the other **25** from the game's own UI. They also square every button and paint the DarkTech skin. `build_release.py` runs all of that once, on a developer's machine, and zips the result (player zip plus a packages-only marketplace suite for the Spiritech Addons page). The Marketplace install is packages only; `install.ps1` also swaps in patched screen layouts, merges skin images into the game's graphics atlas (`cd_image/gui/Default`), and can select the ndGUI providers. It keeps `*.stock` backups of everything it touches. Details are in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) and [docs/marketplace-listing.md](docs/marketplace-listing.md).

## Known limits

- **Waypoints from the Local Map are parked.** The client's native "add waypoint" did nothing in testing. Mission waypoints from the game itself still work.
- The client draws some things itself, and those can't be restyled: Options dropdowns, On/Off toggles, the scale slider.
- Intel only sees chat lines the client delivers to a chat window: a message type you filtered out of every window raises no alert. Loot lines are recognised by their wording (`You received ...`); a wording it doesn't know isn't logged.
- Two backpacks with the same name share one entry in "which character has it".
- Login, character select, character creation and loading screens: the buttons are square, but the art is still stock.
- Inventory list and NCU apply a new column order after a restart. The client builds their rows itself.
- Windows is what's tested. Linux and Mac should work (the tools are plain Python), but nobody has tried yet.
